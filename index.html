I'm interested in the overlap between useful software and weird ideas.

That might mean poking at a security problem, building a tiny automation because a repetitive task annoys me, or making a creative tool that probably didn't need to exist until I thought of it.

I like figuring things out by building, testing, breaking, and trying again. This site is part portfolio, part workbench, and part reminder of what I've been curious about lately.
